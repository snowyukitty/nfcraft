## Summary
A Halloween edition of the pocket NFC workshop: the existing reusable-card sleeve becomes a lantern, and the real emerging card replaces its stem. A plain sleeve control loses the seasonal reading; a ghost-card finalist reads as chat. Widened carved eyes survive native 16px and the Android themed alpha.

## What failed first

The first sleeve's 96-unit eye cuts closed at native 16px. Widening them to
128-unit height and 160-unit minimum width kept two open eyes. A ghost-card
alternative read as a chat bubble, and a bat-cut document lost the reusable
card-holder job. The inherited sleeve and device-free control proved that the
carved face, rather than recolouring, carries the Halloween change.

The icon is an opaque app tile, so its blacked-out tile silhouette is not the
mark review. The separate foreground bake-off and exact themed alpha show the
ownable lantern sleeve and emerging card. The small grin is subordinate;
the two wide eye cuts carry recognition at 16px. Review used IconFlow's
headless contact, neighbourhood and exact-target evidence; no interactive Lab
or independent reviewer is claimed.

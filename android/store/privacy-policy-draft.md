nfcraft writes a web destination you choose to a compatible NFC card. The
Android app does not require an account and has no advertising, analytics,
Internet permission or cloud synchronization.

The current destination, up to 10 recent destinations, and app switches are
saved in the app's private storage on your phone. These settings are not
uploaded by nfcraft. Android cloud backup is disabled for the app. On some
Android 12 or later phones, a direct phone-to-phone transfer can still copy
these settings to your new phone. Card identifiers and
readback results are used locally and shown on screen during an operation;
the app does not persist a card-identifier log or send identifiers to us.

Use Recent → Clear history to delete the recent list. This leaves the current
destination in the input field; delete that text separately if needed. Android
app storage controls can clear all local app settings. Clearing app data does
not erase or change an NFC card.

Writing transfers your chosen URL to the card. Anyone able to read the card
can discover that URL. Do not use URLs containing secrets. A destination
website is separate from nfcraft and handles visitors under its own policies.

The app requests NFC access for reading/writing cards and vibration access for
feedback. It does not request contacts, camera, location or advertising ID.

Contact the developer at the address above for privacy questions. If you
voluntarily send a support message, the chosen email/service provider processes
it; the developer must finalize the support retention/deletion policy before
publication. This Android policy does not make claims about an unbuilt iOS
app or about the separate desktop/public contact-page service.

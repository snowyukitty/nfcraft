# nfcraft icon

Essence: **connect**. Help an operator turn a physical card into a durable connection. Keep the established forest-green and warm-paper workshop palette, with precise, calm geometry.

Four directions were considered: a contact card with a broad tap arc (object/action); a punched registration card (craft metaphor); a dovetail joint (connection through negative space); and a radio coil (system). The coil repeats the generic wireless category; the joint loses the card noun. Finalists compare the first two and a device-free clipped-card control.

The signature hypothesis is a clipped contact card with one broad curved tap impression. No letter tile, chip pins, tiny QR pattern, check badge or generic Wi-Fi fan. The curve is 128 units / two pixels at 16px; the clipped corner is 144 units / 2.25px. The control isolates whether the curve adds recognition. Exact targets: web favicons, Windows ICO (through the desktop target), and transparent tray assets. Sources, receipt and generated output stay together in this repository.

Final: the shorter arc is a real transparent aperture, preserving the cue in color and alpha tray reductions. All six axes scored 4/5 after static-sheet and Review Lab inspection. `check`, receipt-bound `ship`, and case lint pass. Fifteen outputs live under `nfcraft/web/icons/`; `electron` selects desktop formats and adds no Electron runtime. The artwork belongs to the owner, with no IconFlow attribution or commercial restriction.

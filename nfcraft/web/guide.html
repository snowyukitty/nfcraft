BEFORE YOUR FIRST REAL CARD

A few things worth knowing.

What do I need for real cards?

A supported PC/SC reader, compatible NTAG215 cards, native Windows reader access, an approved public HTTPS destination and a phone for acceptance. Reader qualification and a separately approved one-card write come first. Do not infer support from a reader appearing in Windows.

Can I change the profile later?

The card holds a durable URL. You can update the profile behind that URL after an approved deployment. Editing locally does not update the hosted profile. A profile can be shared by many routes; the export review explains that impact.

What protects an unfamiliar card?

Writes require explicit operator approval with a time limit and attempt cap. Unknown content, uncertain geometry and protection settings cause refusal. The app preserves identity after uncertainty and does not permanently lock cards.

Which files are private?

The local journal, raw card UIDs, inventory CSV, backups and operator URL are private. Only the reviewed public manifest is intended for publication, and its profile still needs your approval. Keep the complete application folder when moving the packaged app.

What if something goes wrong?

Pause writes. Keep the card and its assigned identity together. Inspect the card details and recovery instructions; do not reset the database or reuse an uncertain assignment. Back up before migrations. In browser mode, stop the terminal with Ctrl+C.

第一張實體卡之前

先了解這幾件事。

製作實體卡需要什麼？

需要支援的 PC/SC 讀寫器、相容的 NTAG215 卡片、Windows 原生讀寫器存取、核准的公開 HTTPS 目的地，以及驗收用手機。先確認讀寫器資格，再另外批准單張試寫。Windows 看得到裝置，不代表已確認相容。

之後可以更新介紹嗎？

卡片保存長久使用的網址；經核准部署後，可以更新網址背後的介紹。本機修改不會自動更新網站。一份個人資料可能被多個網址共用，匯出確認畫面會說明影響範圍。

遇到不熟悉的卡片，會怎樣？

寫入需要操作者明確批准，並設有時間與次數上限。內容未知、記憶體規格不確定或保護設定不符時，app 會拒絕操作。不確定的卡片仍保留原身份，app 不會永久鎖卡。

哪些資料不能公開？

本機日誌、原始卡片 UID、庫存 CSV、備份與操作者網址都屬於私人資料。只有經確認的公開 manifest 才是預定發布的內容，其中的個人介紹也需要你批准。搬移打包版時，請保留完整 app 資料夾。

操作出問題怎麼辦？

先暫停寫入，保留卡片與它的原身份，查看卡片詳情與復原指引。不要重設資料庫，也不要把不確定的身份分配給其他卡片。遷移前先備份；瀏覽器模式則在終端機按 Ctrl+C 停止。
